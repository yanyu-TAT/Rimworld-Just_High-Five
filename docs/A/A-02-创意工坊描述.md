## 英文版

````text
This mod adds a new social interaction to the game: your colonists can [b]high five[/b] with each other.
Every high five rolls one of four outcomes, each with its own effect.

[hr][/hr]

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*]Four outcomes — bad / plain / good / perfect — each with its own mood and opinion effect.
[*]A perfect high five gives both colonists a random 12-hour buff.
[*]The higher the mutual opinion, the more likely a positive outcome.
[*]Completing a perfect high five rings out a crisp hand-clap and produces a [b]"black flash"[/b] effect — dark red lightning with falling sparks.
[*]Sound volume is adjustable, see below.
[/list]

[h2]Perfect high five[/h2]
A perfect high five always leaves you energized, doesn't it?  
[img]https://images.steamusercontent.com/ugc/13804960243706162555/C03A2E8FAFA4FCC34E3D54F0DE972523FBA167B8/?imw=1280&ima=fit[/img]
Both colonists who complete a perfect high five gain random work speed and move speed bonuses for [b]12 hours[/b].
There is also a 5% chance of receiving a [b]random inspiration[/b] instead.

[h2]Basic rules[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Cooldown:[/b] after a high five, both participants get a 24-hour cooldown. As long as one of the two is ready, they can high five again.
[*][b]Buff stacking:[/b] a higher level replaces the current one; the same or a higher level refreshes the duration; a lower level is ignored.
[*][b]No high fives between enemies[/b] — hostile factions and rivals are excluded.
[*][b]Hands required:[/b] both colonists need at least one usable hand — a prosthetic hand counts too.
[*][b]Carrying someone:[/b] a colonist who is carrying another pawn won't high five.
[/list]

[h2]Options[/h2]
The sound volume is adjustable from [b]0% to 100%[/b], with a test button to preview it.
Open it via [i]Options → Mods → Clap Your Hands → Mod options[/i]. The setting is saved with your other options.

[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]RimWorld 1.6[/b]
[*][b]No Harmony patches at all — no incompatibility issues in theory[/b]
[*]Uses the vanilla mod options menu, so no extra libraries are needed
[*]Performance friendly
[/list]

[h2]Languages[/h2]
English and Simplified Chinese.

[h2]Source & Feedback[/h2]
Source code, bug reports and suggestions:
[url=https://github.com/yanyu-TAT/Rimworld-Clap_Your_Hands]GitHub — Rimworld-Clap_Your_Hands[/url]
````

## 中文版

````text
这个模组向游戏中添加了一种新的社交互动：殖民者之间可以[b]击掌[/b]。
每次击掌会随机产生四种结果之一，并根据结果产生不同效果。

[hr][/hr]

[h2]功能[/h2]
[list]
[*]四种结果：糟糕 / 普通 / 不错 / 完美，各自带来不同的心情与好感度变化。
[*]完美击掌为双方各提供一份随机的 12 小时增益。
[*]双方好感度越高，越容易打出正面结果。
[*]完成完美击掌时，会响起清脆的拍手声，并产生[b]“黑闪”[/b]特效——暗红闪电与溅落的火花。
[*]音效音量可调，见下。
[/list]

[h2]完美击掌[/h2]
完美的击掌总是让人动力满满，不是吗？  
[img]https://images.steamusercontent.com/ugc/13804960243706162555/C03A2E8FAFA4FCC34E3D54F0DE972523FBA167B8/?imw=1280&ima=fit[/img]
完成完美击掌的双方将各获得随机程度的工作速度加成和移速加成，持续 [b]12 小时[/b]。
另有 5% 概率改为获得一次[b]随机灵感[/b]。

[h2]一些基础设定：[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]冷却：[/b]击掌后双方各进入 24 小时冷却；只要两人中有一人冷却完毕，即可再次击掌。
[*][b]增益叠加：[/b]更高等级覆盖当前等级；同级或更高级会重置持续时间；更低等级被忽略。
[*][b]敌对与仇视不互动：[/b]敌对派系与仇视关系会被排除。
[*][b]需要手：[/b]双方都至少需要一只可用的手——假手当然也是手。
[*][b]手上抱着人时不击掌：[/b]正抱着其他小人的殖民者不会击掌。
[/list]

[h2]设置[/h2]
音效音量可在 [b]0% ~ 100%[/b] 之间调节，并提供试听按钮。
入口：[i]选项 → 模组 → Clap Your Hands → 模组选项[/i]。设置会与其他选项一同保存。

[h2]兼容性[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]RimWorld 1.6[/b]
[*][b]无任何Harmony Patch，理论上不存在不兼容[/b]
[*]设置页使用原版模组选项，无需任何额外库
[*]性能友好
[/list]

[h2]语言[/h2]
简体中文与 English。

[h2]源码与反馈[/h2]
源码、问题反馈与建议：
[url=https://github.com/yanyu-TAT/Rimworld-Clap_Your_Hands]GitHub — Rimworld-Clap_Your_Hands[/url]
````
