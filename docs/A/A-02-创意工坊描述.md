## 英文版

````text
[b]Clap Your Hands[/b] adds a new social interaction to the game: your colonists can [b]high five[/b] with each other. Every high five rolls one of four outcomes, each with its own effect.

[hr][/hr]

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*]Four outcomes — bad / plain / good / perfect — each with its own mood and opinion effect.
[*]A perfect high five gives both colonists a random 12-hour buff.
[*]The higher the mutual opinion, the more likely a positive outcome.
[/list]

[h2]Perfect high five reward[/h2]
Both colonists gain random work speed and move speed bonuses for [b]12 hours[/b].
There is also a 5% chance of receiving a [b]random inspiration[/b] instead.

[h2]Basic rules[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Cooldown:[/b] after a high five, both participants get a 24-hour cooldown. As long as one of the two is ready, they can high five again.
[*][b]Buff stacking:[/b] a higher level replaces the current one; the same or a higher level refreshes the duration; a lower level is ignored.
[*][b]No high fives between enemies[/b] — hostile factions and rivals are excluded.
[*][b]Hands required:[/b] both colonists need at least one usable hand — a prosthetic hand counts too.
[/list]

[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]RimWorld 1.6[/b]
[*][b]No Harmony patches at all — no incompatibility issues in theory[/b]
[*]Performance friendly
[/list]

[h2]Languages[/h2]
English and Simplified Chinese.

[h2]Source & Feedback[/h2]
Source code, bug reports and suggestions:
[url=https://github.com/yanyu-TAT/Rimworld-Clap_Your_Hands]GitHub — Rimworld-Clap_Your_Hands[/url]
````

## 中文版

````text
[b]Clap Your Hands[/b] 向游戏中添加了一种新的社交互动：殖民者之间可以[b]击掌[/b]。每次击掌会随机产生四种结果之一，并根据结果产生不同效果。

[hr][/hr]

[h2]功能[/h2]
[list]
[*]四种结果：糟糕 / 普通 / 不错 / 完美，各自带来不同的心情与好感度变化。
[*]完美击掌为双方各提供一份随机的 12 小时增益。
[*]双方好感度越高，越容易打出正面结果。
[/list]

[h2]完美击掌的奖励[/h2]
双方各获得随机程度的工作速度加成和移速加成，持续 [b]12 小时[/b]。
另有 5% 概率改为获得一次[b]随机灵感[/b]。

[h2]一些基础设定：[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]冷却：[/b]击掌后双方各进入 24 小时冷却；只要两人中有一人冷却完毕，即可再次击掌。
[*][b]增益叠加：[/b]更高等级覆盖当前等级；同级或更高级会重置持续时间；更低等级被忽略。
[*][b]敌对与仇视不互动：[/b]敌对派系与仇视关系会被排除。
[*][b]需要手：[/b]双方都至少需要一只可用的手——假手当然也是手。
[/list]

[h2]兼容性[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]RimWorld 1.6[/b]
[*][b]无任何Harmony Patch，理论上不存在不兼容[/b]
[*]性能友好
[/list]

[h2]语言[/h2]
简体中文与 English。

[h2]源码与反馈[/h2]
源码、问题反馈与建议：
[url=https://github.com/yanyu-TAT/Rimworld-Clap_Your_Hands]GitHub — Rimworld-Clap_Your_Hands[/url]
````
